It automatically repairs self-intersections, handles multipart geometries safely, and guarantees area equity (CV <0.01%) for cadastral, agricultural, and experimental design workflows. Integrated into QGIS Processing Toolbox for batch and model workflows. Concept initiated 2026-05-15.
